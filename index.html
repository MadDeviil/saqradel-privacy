Privacy Policy

Autoreply — last updated August 9, 2026

Autoreply is a private tool operated by Omar Abdulmajeed to reply to comments left on his own social media content and to send the requested information as a direct message. It runs on the accounts listed below and on no others. It is not offered as a product or service to any other person or business, and it never acts on content owned by anyone else.

Data we process

When you comment on a post or reel published by one of the accounts above, the tool processes only the following:

  • Your username or display name, and the numeric ID the platform assigns you
  • The text of your comment, its ID, and the time it was posted
  • The ID of the post or reel you commented on
  • The keyword your comment matched, and the delivery status of the reply (sent / pending / could not be delivered)

We do not collect your email address, phone number, payment details, or location, and we do not collect any data beyond the comment itself. We do not read your profile, your other posts, or anything you have written elsewhere.

Why we process it

  • To post a public reply beneath your comment
  • To send you, by direct message, the content you asked for
  • To make sure the same person is never sent the same message twice

The tool never initiates a conversation on its own. Every message it sends is a direct response to a comment you chose to write, in line with Meta's rules for private replies. If your settings prevent us from messaging you, the tool says so publicly under your comment once and does nothing further unless you write to us first.

Storage and retention

Data is stored in a local SQLite database on a machine controlled solely by the account owner. It is not sent to any cloud service or third party, is never used for advertising, profiling or analytics, and is never sold or shared with anyone.

It is kept for a maximum of 12 months and then deleted. It can be deleted sooner at your request.

Your rights and data deletion

At any time, you may request to:

  • See what data is held about you
  • Have your data permanently deleted
  • Stop receiving any messages from these accounts

To make a request, send a direct message to @saqradel.marketing on Instagram or to the Saqr Adel - Media Buyer Page on Facebook, or email privacy@saqradel.com. Requests are handled within 30 days at the latest.

You can also revoke the app's access yourself at any time:

  • Instagram — Settings → Apps and websites
  • Facebook — Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites

Children

This tool is not directed at anyone under 13, and we do not knowingly process their data.
