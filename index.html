Privacy Policy

Autoreply — last updated August 8, 2026

Autoreply is a private tool used by the owner of the Instagram account @saqradel.marketing to reply to comments left on that account's own posts and to send the requested information as a direct message. It operates on a single Instagram account and is not offered as a service to third parties.

Data we process

When you comment on a post from this account, the tool processes only the following:

  • Your Instagram username and numeric user ID
  • The text of your comment, its ID, and the time it was posted
  • The ID of the post you commented on
  • The keyword your comment matched, and the delivery status of the reply (sent / pending)

We do not collect your email address, phone number, payment details, or location, and we do not collect any data beyond the comment itself.

Why we process it

  • To post a public reply beneath your comment
  • To send you, by direct message, the content you asked for
  • To make sure the same comment is never answered more than once

The tool never initiates a conversation on its own. Every message it sends is a direct response to a comment you chose to write, in line with Meta's rules for private replies.

Storage and retention

Data is stored in a local SQLite database on a machine controlled solely by the account owner. It is not sent to any cloud service or third party, is never used for advertising or analytics, and is never sold or shared with anyone.

It is kept for a maximum of 12 months and then deleted. It can be deleted sooner at your request.

Your rights and data deletion

At any time, you may request to:

  • See what data is held about you
  • Have your data permanently deleted
  • Stop receiving any messages from the account

To make a request, send a direct message to @saqradel.marketing on Instagram, or email privacy@saqradel.com. Requests are handled within 30 days at the latest.

You can also revoke the app's access at any time from your Instagram account settings: Settings → Apps and websites.

Children

This tool is not directed at anyone under 13, and we do not knowingly process their data.

Changes to this policy

If this page changes, the "last updated" date above is revised accordingly.

Contact: privacy@saqradel.com — or a direct message to @saqradel.marketing on Instagram.
